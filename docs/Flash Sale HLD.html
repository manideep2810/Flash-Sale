Failure paths

Hold expires

Sweeper finds score < now in the holds ZSET and runs release.lua. Stock returns; I1 holds.

Payment declined

Saga moves PAYMENT_PENDING → PAYMENT_FAILED → RELEASED. No charge, stock returns; I1 and I3 hold.

Relay crashes

Un-acked entries stay in the Redis stream. On restart the relay re-sends them; consumers dedupe by eventId.
